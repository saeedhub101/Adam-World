# Adam-City reference archive — provenance and limits

This directory preserves selected assets from `saeedhub101/Adam-City`. It is a reference archive, not an automatic runtime source for Adam World. Keep Manila/Rizal Park material in its regional context.

## Manila / Ermita reference files

- OSM building-detail GeoJSON/CSV: contributor-supplied IDs, raw tags and geometry. Licence: ODbL 1.0; preserve attribution and share-alike obligations.
- Evidence-link CSV: source/photo candidates and notes. A candidate link is not a verified facade assignment; inspect each photo's own licence.
- Landmark study JSON: architectural research. Its recorded status says geometry matching is pending; do not attach its facade profile to an unverified footprint.
- Procedural facade reports/indices: historical generated outputs. Their windows, doors, balconies, colours and signs are approximations/placeholders, not measured building details.

## Rizal Park / Luneta

Official source references include NPDC's downloads, park page, tree inventory, technical map and published tree-distribution materials. The preserved CSV is a partial export, not a complete park-wide tree inventory. Coordinate conversions must retain original DMS values and source-row provenance. Landmark labels without verified coordinates remain research notes, not map features.

## Exclusions and use policy

- Do not treat unverified KartaView or other nearby imagery as a confirmed facade.
- Do not present procedural details as real measurements.
- Do not merge Manila coordinates into other cities.
- Preserve source IDs, dates, URLs, licences and transformation notes.
- Validate geometry and coordinate placement before any runtime integration.
