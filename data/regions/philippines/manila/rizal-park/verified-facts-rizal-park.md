# Rizal Park and Ermita — source-linked research notes

**Status:** research notes, not a complete GIS inventory.  
**Last checked in source record:** 2026-10-18.  
**Purpose:** preserve official facts and outstanding verification needs before any runtime mapping.

## Park management and official records

Use NPDC publications for park management, named sections, technical maps and tree inventory. A published park-level fact does not establish a coordinate for each individual tree, monument or facility.

## Tree information

The 2024 inventory/export retained in the project is partial. Keep common name, scientific name, source row, source ID and coordinate provenance distinct. Do not infer a scientific name from a common name or distribute an aggregate tree count across guessed positions.

## Named landmarks and sections

Central and western section names transcribed from official maps are research labels until their exact map positions are digitized and checked. Do not create point coordinates from the text label alone.

## Geography and street-map context

OSM and official park mapping can provide complementary geometry. Keep their IDs and attribution separate. A map label is not proof of the exact boundary or entrance position.

## Known gaps

- Complete park-wide tree rows and verified point coordinates.
- Exact coordinates for every named landmark/park feature.
- Cross-checking the technical map against current park geometry.
- Per-feature licence/attribution review before redistributing any image.

## Data and rights

Retain original files and source URLs. Record transformations and coordinate conversions. This research document does not authorize use of a restricted image or certify the completeness of the current Android game.
